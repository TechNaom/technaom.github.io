# Brief: Personal website

**Reconstructed 2026-11-27** from the live site and its code; lines marked *(inferred)* were not read from a
document. The 3D feature lane is at the end.

**One line:** The public home of Manohar Papasani's AI R&D lab: it shows the digital workforce (41 agents in
9 teams) at work, with every number checked against one facts file.

## The problem *(inferred)*
People who meet the owner on LinkedIn (recruiters, clients, funders, engineers) cannot see in one place what
the lab builds, how it runs, or whether the numbers are real.

## Who it is for *(inferred)*
- **Users:** recruiters and hiring managers, possible clients and funders, engineers following the work.
- **Buyer:** none; it is the owner's own site.

## What changes when it works
A visitor understands in a minute that the agents do real work every day, and can check the evidence.

## First version (built, live since 2026-10-26)
Seven pages (home, workforce, architecture, growth, lab notebook, work, about), facts from one file, a copy
gate that blocks any wrong number or poor sentence, and brand numbers that the analytics agents update.

## Feature lane: 3D neural network website (2026-11-27)
**Why:** the owner wants the site to feel like an AI lab you walk into, not a page you read: "floating
in the universe", but AI-centric. The owner chose a **living neural network**: the lab is a brain, each
team is a cluster of nodes, each agent is a node joined by glowing links, and a post fires as a signal
along the links.

**First version of the lane:** one 3D network behind all 7 pages; each section picks a reusable view
(the templates); the home page tells the story in 3D (the network, the teams on a 24-hour dial, one post's
journey to five platforms); dark only; the text always readable and loaded first.

**Not in the lane:** a light version, a public template gallery, an open-source template kit, AI-video frames.

## How we will know it works *(proposed numbers)*
- The owner says the 3D site is better than today's, after UAT on their own phone and laptop.
- A mid-range phone shows the page text within 2.5 seconds; the 3D never blocks reading.
- Every gate stays green: type check, build, the copy gate, and the analytics agents' automatic deploys.
