    Reel 5 · The journey

    From a farming village to a digital workforce

    I build software that does real work. For more than ten years that meant automation, data platforms and production systems. Today it means AI agents: digital employees that research, write, check and publish on their own.

    1. Roots

      A farming village

      Grew up in Komarapudi, near Nellore, in a family of farmers. Education was the way forward, up to an MCA from Sri Venkateswara University.

    2. 2010 – 2019

      The builder years

      Hyderabad: Python automation at MoSys, Java platforms at Pyramid Softsol, Spark and Scala pipelines on AWS at Virtusa.

    3. 2021 – 2026

      Automation at scale

      Five years at Bank of America: SRE and DevOps, then GenAI for a 100–150 person organisation, and the prompt-engineering team I founded.

    4. 2026 – now

      A digital workforce

      An agent factory for a 20-agent programme at ValueMomentum; now leading agentic GenAI on AWS at Minfy, and running this lab on the side.

    The question changed from "can AI help an engineer?" to "can AI do the job, reliably, every day?"

    Teaching, all along. A trainer since 2004 and once an Assistant Professor: 500+ engineers trained, and a learning group in every company I join. The free courses here are the same habit.
